Qlakaari Interior is a creative interior design startup focused on creating modern, practical, and budget-conscious spaces. The studio combines aesthetics, comfort, functionality, and personalization to create thoughtfully designed interiors.

Qlakaari emphasizes building long-term relationships through design excellence, working with individuals and partners who value thoughtful design. Whether it's a dedicated kitchen remodel, a luxury bathroom transformation, or a complete home addition, every space is crafted with precision and care.
